Why Friday

Not a chatbot. A colleague with hands.

Your best people spend the day on coordination instead of the work only they can do. Friday takes the repetitive, predictable part — and does it.

Capabilities

What she does

Friday connects to email, calendar, documents, spreadsheets and file storage through secure, scoped access.

Communications

  • Reads, sorts and prioritises the inbox; drafts replies in your voice; escalates only what needs you.
  • Structured, on-brand mail — invitations, notices, follow-ups — with a plain-text fallback.
  • Group sends with one visible recipient and everyone else in blind copy; bounces caught and fixed.
  • Voice calls to remind, nudge or follow up at the time you ask.

Operations and scheduling

  • Schedules, reschedules and cancels meetings, with video links and access for the right people.
  • Reminders to participants at the interval you choose.
  • Recurring routines — morning brief, end-of-day summary, weekly review — configured once.
  • Chases pending work until it is delivered, then stops on its own.

Data, documents and money

  • Master sheets for customers, candidates, vendors and field partners — built and kept current.
  • Repairs messy exports: mangled phone numbers, dates stored as serial numbers.
  • Proposals, briefings, reports and decks, delivered as PDF and editable files.
  • GST-compliant invoices, numbered in sequence, logged and never re-used.

Sales, field teams and the market

  • Onboards field partners and keeps the pipeline live, from application to activation.
  • Collects status by mail, chat or a call, with every attempt logged.
  • Monitors competitors and products, and reports only material changes, with the source named.
  • Reads contracts, tenders and CVs, and pulls out the obligations, dates and names that matter.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Friday?

Friday is an AI colleague from CybroXide Technologies. She has her own email address and phone number, works inside the tools your team already uses, does the work — mail, meetings, sheets, calls — and keeps a full log of what she did.

We already use ChatGPT. How is Friday different?

You use ChatGPT as a tool. Friday is a colleague: her own mailbox, her own number, and she executes and logs the work instead of suggesting it.

Is our data safe?

Your accounts, your data. Access is scoped, credentials are stored encrypted and revocable at any time, and nothing is sold or used for training.

How do I know she did not do something wrong?

Every action is logged with a timestamp. Money, contracts and group mail need your approval before anything leaves.

Will my team have to adopt another app?

No. They message her on the channel they already use, and mostly they do not have to message at all.

Who fixes it when it gets something wrong?

She tells you it failed, why, and what she did next. That is a rule, not a feature.

How much does Friday cost?

Pricing is quoted per deployment: a monthly subscription per assistant, a one-time setup and configuration, and usage such as calls and messages at cost, with volume terms from five seats. Ask for the current commercial sheet.

How do we start?

With a 14-day pilot, not a contract. Pick three workflows that already cost you hours every week; she runs them in your accounts and you receive a weekly written report and the full action log. Then keep it, expand it, or walk away.
