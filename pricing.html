Questions

Pricing and pilot questions

How is Friday priced?

Pricing is quoted per deployment: a monthly subscription per assistant, a one-time setup and configuration, and usage such as calls and messages at cost, with volume terms from five seats. Talk to us for the current commercial sheet.

How does the 14-day pilot work?

You pick three workflows that already cost your team hours every week. We agree them on day one, configure and test them, and Friday runs them in your accounts. You receive a weekly written report and the full action log.

Do we need to replace our existing tools?

No. Friday works inside the email, calendar, documents, spreadsheets and file storage your team already uses, and your team messages her on the channel it already uses. There is no new app to adopt.

How long does setup take?

The pilot plan is days, not quarters: workflows are agreed on day one, configured and tested over the next two days, and running in your accounts from day four. The exact timing depends on the workflows and access involved.

What happens after the pilot?

You decide: keep it, expand it to more workflows or seats, or walk away. The pilot is not a contract.

Can Friday work with our existing workflows?

That is the starting point. Discovery begins with the work your team already does — follow-ups, status collection, scheduling and records — and Friday takes on the repetitive, predictable part of it. When a task needs a human, she says so.

Ready to see where Friday can help?

Tell us about your team and the work that eats the day. We’ll suggest the workflows to start with and share the current commercial sheet.
